\section{Conclusion}

We introduced PLRE and VIGIL, whose extracted treatment layer gates retrieval by operative authority. On held-out pairs, gating lifts PLRE F1 by 0.06 and cuts stale citations from 12.7\% to 4.1\% relative to flat similarity. The layer reaches negative-treatment micro-F1 0.80 on gold spans and 0.81 against a commercial citator.
