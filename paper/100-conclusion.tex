\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \plre{} and \sysname{}, whose extracted treatment layer gates retrieval by operative authority.
On held-out pairs, gating lifts \plre{} F1 by 0.06 and cuts stale citations from 12.7\% to 4.1\% relative to flat similarity; these are point estimates whose confidence intervals, together with a non-distractor and a date-filter comparison, are the next step.
The layer reaches negative-treatment micro-F1 0.80 on gold spans and 0.81 against a commercial citator.
