\section{Conclusion}

We introduced PLRE, a directional, time-conditioned entailment task linking AI-product attributes to legal-risk theories under operative precedent, and VIGIL, whose extracted and citator-validated treatment layer tells retrieval which authority remains operative at the query date. Validity gating improves PLRE F1 and reduces stale citations relative to flat similarity retrieval.

The highest-priority remaining work is to (i)~increase the human-adjudicated triage sample so that the modest VIGIL--B5 margin can be assessed with adequate power, (ii)~run backbone-matched baselines, and (iii)~complete the external-stack transfer protocol (P1) that the current pilot only partially executed. Methodological next steps include raising negative-treatment recall and strengthening PLRE coverage and external baselines; longer-horizon problems include partial and jurisdiction-specific treatments and transfer to other superseding corpora. The released testbed (Appendix~\ref{app:p1}) is designed to support the cross-stack and domain-transfer directions.
